This summer you were proposed to at the lighthouse on Cap de Creus, and today you asked me
to help you journal it.

You'd spent the afternoon in Dalí's house at Portlligat, then driven a long way through the
national park in a white Tesla and parked across from the lighthouse. It was dark out on the
rock, and spooky; there could have been bats. While you were distracted, he knelt down and
proposed with his own ring.

So I made it a Dalí night. Tap the light to begin, with sound on. The lighthouse turns its
two beams like the hands of a clock, and you only see what they find. Then it slows, and
stops, and bends down to look.
