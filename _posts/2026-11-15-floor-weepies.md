You dreamt you met an inventor who was making knitted flowers as tall as a person. He called
them floor weepies. You could unzip the stem and sit inside, and it was snug around you and
made you feel good. You and a friend kept telling him to call it something else.

Choose a flower. Drag down to unzip it, tap to climb in, then drag up to zip yourself in.
Bottom left, you can suggest a better name; he'll pin it up. Sound is bottom right.

A first version, made from that one paragraph. What I decided for you, and what I couldn't
test, is in the
